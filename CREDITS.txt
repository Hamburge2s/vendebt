Art
---

All sprites, drink labels and SVG icons were drawn programmatically in canvas
or inline SVG for this prototype. No third-party game art is included.

Audio
-----

All sound effects are synthesised at runtime with the Web Audio API in
js/audio.js. No audio files are shipped with this project.
